## 2. What the baseline choice does (read this before Stage 4)

The two offsets differ only in each group's log-odds baseline. The cohort offset is constant within group across waves. Changing from the pool to the cohort therefore shifts each group's coefficient by the difference in offset gaps:

$$\beta'_g = \beta_g + \left[\text{offset}^{pool}_g - \text{offset}^{pool}_C\right] - \left[\text{offset}^{cohort}_g - \text{offset}^{cohort}_C\right]$$

For No Religion, the pool share is about 9% (Figure 1 range 7–11%) and the cohort share is 12.3%. The Catholic shares are about 80% (pool) and 73% (cohort). Together these produce a shift of about 0.74 log-odds, which takes the No Religion contrast from +0.55 to −0.19. The audit's cohort column is consistent with this calculation. Protestant and Other Religion shift in the same way.

Consequences:

- **The primary-specification headline changes.** Under the cohort primary, only Other Religion (19 egos in the regression sample) has a positive contrast with Catholic. No Religion is negative and Protestant is about zero. The abstract's claim that non-Catholic groups collectively exceed Catholics does not hold as written under the primary. A rough tie-weighted average of the audit's cohort coefficients is about +0.07, which is close to zero. This needs to be confirmed by a direct fit (Stage 3, headline indicator model).
- **Yule's Q itself does not depend on the baseline.** Observed Q is computed from the tie table alone, so Figure 2 and the observed Q columns of Table 2 are unchanged. Only the permutation null depends on the baseline, so Code 07 needs a cohort-based null.
- **Table 3 Panels A–B and Figure 3 do depend on the baseline.** The audit listed Panels A–B as unaffected. That holds only under the pool baseline, so the audit's "unaffected" list must be revised.
- **The cohort baseline is a recruited sample, not the student body.** The 718 egos with religion known come from snowball-based recruitment covering about 36% of the entering class. The cohort offset is therefore an approximation, and the manuscript must say so.
- **Group×wave slopes are not affected by the cohort offset.** Because the cohort offset is constant within group, it moves main effects but not the group×wave slopes. Wave trends are then identified only from the outcome.
- **A free offset coefficient is not identified under the cohort baseline.** Under that baseline the offset is collinear with the group dummies. The check is reported only under the pool baseline, where wave variation exists but is small.
